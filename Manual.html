DIFUSION BOXES GUIDE

SECTION V: THALAMIC MANIFOLD

To prevent a "premature thermal event," the Pilot must identify the quantity of wires and their specific color-ordering. Count from top to bottom.
- 3 Wires: If there are no Red wires, cut the second wire. Otherwise, if the last wire is Blue, cut the last wire. If there is more than one Green wire, cut the first Green wire. Otherwise, cut the middle wire.
- 4 Wires: If there is more than one Red wire and the serial number’s last digit is odd, cut the last Red wire. Otherwise, if the last wire is Green and there are no Red wires, cut the first wire. If there is exactly one Blue wire, cut the first wire. Otherwise, cut the second wire.
- 5 Wires: If the last wire is Blue and the serial number’s last digit is odd, cut the fourth wire. Otherwise, if there is exactly one Red wire and there is more than one Green wire, cut the first wire. If there are no Red wires, cut the second wire.

SECTION VI: CRITICALITY STABILIZATION FORMULA

The stabilization keypad requires a 4-digit input derived from the "Verrone Isotope Constant." Locate the 4-digit Serial Number on the nuke’s chassis.
1. Take the First Digit and multiply it by your Pilot Rank (4).
2. Add the Second Digit to the result.
3. Subtract the Fourth Digit.
4. If the resulting number is less than 10, add 14.
5. Multiply the final result by the Third Digit of the serial number.
6. Input only the last four digits of your final calculation into the keypad.

SECTION IX: RADIOLOGICAL EXPOSURE & THE "FEVER"

Should the Pilot successfully deploy or neutralize their payload, they must immediately assess their biological integrity. Uranium Fever is not a choice; it is a consequence of service to Verrone.
- Stage 1 (Nausea/Metallic Taste): Standard operational side effect. Continue flight path. Do not adjust oxygen mixture.

- Stage 3 (Auditory Hallucinations): If you hear the voices of the Empire of Farnesia whispering through the fuselage, disconnect your headset. They are ghosts of a dead era. They cannot hurt the armor of a Verrone vessel.
